Before you write to us

How do I track my order?

Open the Stackly app or website, go to Orders and tap Track. You will see your rider live on the map.

How do I get a refund?

Report the issue within 2 hours of delivery with a photo. Approved refunds reach you in 3 to 5 working days.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Orders can be changed or cancelled before the kitchen starts preparing them. After that, please contact support.

How do I partner as a kitchen?

Write to partners@stacklyeats.in or select Partner with us in the form. We will schedule a visit and tasting.
